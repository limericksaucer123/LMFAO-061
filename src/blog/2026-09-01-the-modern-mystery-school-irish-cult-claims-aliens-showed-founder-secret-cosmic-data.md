## **Ian O'Grady is a sci-fi and horror writer from Limerick but presently living in Cork.** 

## **In 2021 , Ian produced an article for Irish Cult watchdog** *[**'**Dialogue Ireland'](https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2021/05/23/modern-mystery-school-money-magic-sex-and-power/)* **about The Modern Mystery School , an international cult which blends Christian mysticism and ritual magic from the Hermetic Order of The Golden Dawn (A late 19th century Occult group whose members included W.B Yeats and Maud Gonne).**

## **The article was published in response to a *[Vice article from 2021.](https://www.vice.com/en/article/inside-the-bizarre-cult-whose-members-allege-sexual-and-financial-exploitation/)* Allegedly , the founder of Dialogue Ireland was the first to bring the cult to the publics attention and collaborated with Vice on the piece but was not credited. As part of his research , Ian joined the cult but found that membership and entry to anything past zoom calls was very expensive. This piece is an introduction to the MMS and an update on thee groups current state in Ireland.** 

![](/images/modern-mystery-image-3.jpg)

When I last wrote about the Modern Mystery School back in 2021, their presence in Ireland  was minimal, consisting only of a few quiet, scattered practitioners whispering about sacred  geometry in private rooms. Yet beneath the surface, that quiet period was a chance for the  school to regroup between Dublin and Derry. Today, those sporadic meetups have  transformed into a highly coordinated step-by-step system of initiation that begins with a  single, one-on-one Life Activation session promised to unlock a seeker's hidden divine  potential. Once that door is open, students are seamlessly swept into the high-energy  mystique of the Empower Thyself weekend initiation. It is a glossy, mesmerizing reboot, but  beneath this modern wellness branding lies a complex, high-control esoteric architecture that  stretches far beyond our local shores. 

To fully understand the foundation of the school, you have to look at a pivotal close  encounter that serves as their core origin story. According to founder Gudni Gudnason, he  and his ex-wife, Laurie Gudnason, were driving down an isolated highway in Idaho on May  21, 1998, when they pulled over by a lake. It was here that they experienced a direct contact  event with the "Nathors," an advanced race of interdimensional beings. Gudni recounts  witnessing a blinding light as an off-world "Plasma Ship" entered the water, noting its  striking resemblance to the plasma craft captured in photographs near Mexico City around  that same time. Gudni asserts that while he already possessed ancient lineage tools to activate  22 of humanity's 24 spiritual and physical DNA strands, this encounter provided a critical  missing piece. The beings supposedly transmitted the cosmic data required to unlock the final  two strands, fundamentally shifting his understanding of human potential and cementing his  role as a bridge between Earth and broader galactic intelligences. 

Beyond this cosmic framework of off-world contact, the school relies heavily on a much  more earthbound occult tradition: the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. Founded in late  19th-century Britain, the Golden Dawn famously packed Western ceremonial magic,  Kabbalah, and astral travel into a rigid, step-by-step initiatory system. Gudnason claims a  direct personal link here, asserting he studied within the Golden Dawn system for years  before launching his own path. Walk into their Irish curriculum today, and this historical  footprint is everywhere. As students advance, they are systematically taught core Golden  Dawn rituals and elemental magic, just repackaged under a modern label.  But the magical blending doesn't stop with 19th-century occultism; it also borrows heavily  from traditional Christianity. For those who climb high enough into the school's "Ritual  Master" tracks, the curriculum shifts into a surprising mix of Christian rituals, including  formal training in exorcisms. Students are taught explicit strategies to engage hidden negative  forces, learn the outer signs of demonic possession, and perform sacred methods to clear  entities. They are even instructed on how to craft Holy Water, mix Holy Oil, and partake in a  specialized version of the Holy Sacrament to sanctify spaces. 

Instead of a casual path where you just do your own thing, everything here is strictly  gatekept. Advanced secrets, special rituals, and higher titles are locked tightly behind  successive tiers that you have to unlock one by one. Even though these rituals are mostly  lifted from old history books and religious traditions, members find themselves trapped in a  rigid ladder where they can only grow spiritually by moving up the school's internal ranks.  Ultimately, it ensures your entire spiritual development is completely dependent on the  authority of the hierarchy. 

![](/images/modern-mystery-image-2.jpg)

Unlocking those gatekept tiers comes with a massive price tag. Moving up the school's strict  spiritual ladder is not just about dedication because it requires a serious financial investment.  It all starts out relatively small with an initial Life Activation session costing a couple  hundred euros. This is quickly followed by the Empower Thyself initiation weekend, which  usually runs around several hundred more. But once a seeker is hooked and wants to ascend  into the advanced ranks, the numbers skyrocket. To reach the coveted Ritual Master track,  where those intense exorcism rituals, Holy Water crafting, and higher-tier secrets are finally  handed down, students have to shell out thousands of euros. They must pay for mandatory  international seminars, specialized tools, and ongoing recertifications. This financial blueprint  turns spiritual progression into an expensive and exclusive pipeline, ensuring that only those  who can continuously fund the hierarchy are allowed to reach the top. 

Once a student pays their way into this exclusive Ritual Master bracket, the training shifts  from passive energy work into what the school frames as active spiritual warfare. At this  level, initiates are officially designated as warriors tasking themselves with confronting and  fighting actual demonic forces on the astral plane. To prepare them for these supernatural  battles, the school introduces a highly unique physical requirement: Gudni’s own specialized  blend of spiritual martial arts. Taught through programs like the Warriors of Light, this  training combines rigorous physical conditioning with esoteric combat techniques. Gudni  claims to have mastered these skills during a mystical journey to a remote Tibetan monastery,  and the school uses this martial arts curriculum to teach Ritual Masters how to defend their  energy fields and physically channel cosmic power. By blending martial arts with advanced  exorcism magic, the hierarchy ensures that its highest-paying members see themselves not  just as students, but as literal protectors fighting a cosmic war to save the planet.  

But the training eventually takes a much darker, grueling turn as students try to advance to  full initiation at the third-step Ritual Master level. According to accounts from survivors and  investigative research, this tier moves away from standard classroom workshops and into  intense psychological and physical endurance tests. Initiates are subjected to an exhausting  four-day ordeal that involves calorie restriction and forced starvation, a tactic designed to  completely drain their physical energy and break down mental resistance. By the time the  final phase arrives, the students are completely exhausted and vulnerable, setting the stage for 

the school's ultimate test of submission: being buried alive. In extreme rituals, such as those  conducted at high-tier locations like Mount Fuji, initiates have been physically buried in the  earth as a symbolic death and a brutal test of faith. Because they are too mentally and  physically broken to refuse, students submit to the burial, cementing absolute obedience to  the leadership before they are finally "reborn" into the school's highest secret ranks. 

It is this profound disconnect that makes the group's current Irish revival so critical to track.  When Dialogue Ireland first began monitoring the Modern Mystery School back in 2012, the  initial red flags were already raised over financial pressure and intensive recruitment tactics.  

If you scroll through the social media pages of the Dublin and Derry teams today, however,  you will not see any mention of interdimensional plasma ships, 19th-century magical  gatekeeping, or high-tier starvation tests and earth burials. Instead, you will find bright,  modern wellness imagery, smiling faces at local charity runs, and gentle invitations to unlock  your inner potential through a simple Life Activation session. But as the breadcrumbs lead  from the entry-level workshops straight to Gudni Gudnason's ultimate cosmic hierarchy, the  reality becomes clear. The quiet period that followed the public exposés has ended, and the  Modern Mystery School is once again expanding its footprint in Ireland. They have simply  mastered the art of hiding a complex, high-control esoteric world behind a very glossy,  ordinary-looking door.
